# Personas

## Primary Personas

### 1. HR Administrator

**Role**: Manages employee lifecycle and HR operations.

**Goals**:
- Efficiently manage employee records
- Enforce company policies
- Generate HR reports
- Ensure compliance

**Pain Points**:
- Manual data entry across systems
- Difficulty tracking policy compliance
- Limited visibility into organizational health
- Time-consuming approval processes

**OrgSphere Value**:
- Centralized employee management
- Automated policy enforcement
- Real-time organizational insights
- Streamlined approval workflows

---

### 2. Manager

**Role**: Leads a team and manages daily operations.

**Goals**:
- Manage team members effectively
- Approve requests quickly
- Understand team capacity
- Support team growth

**Pain Points**:
- Unclear reporting structures
- Slow approval processes
- Difficulty tracking team availability
- Limited visibility into team skills

**OrgSphere Value**:
- Visual team structure
- Quick approval workflows
- Real-time availability tracking
- Skills and capacity insights

---

### 3. Employee

**Role**: Individual contributor.

**Goals**:
- Manage own profile and requests
- Understand place in organization
- Collaborate with team
- Grow career

**Pain Points**:
- Hard to find the right person for questions
- Slow request processing
- Unclear reporting lines
- Limited self-service options

**OrgSphere Value**:
- Clear organizational visibility
- Fast request processing
- Self-service operations
- Career development insights

---

### 4. Executive (C-Suite)

**Role**: Strategic leadership.

**Goals**:
- Understand organizational health
- Make data-driven decisions
- Plan for growth
- Ensure efficiency

**Pain Points**:
- Limited real-time insights
- Difficulty visualizing structure
- Unclear resource allocation
- Slow reporting

**OrgSphere Value**:
- Real-time dashboards
- Visual organizational charts
- Resource analytics
- AI-powered insights

---

### 5. IT Administrator

**Role**: Manages technology and integrations.

**Goals**:
- Ensure system security
- Manage integrations
- Support users
- Maintain system health

**Pain Points**:
- Complex integration requirements
- Security compliance burden
- User management overhead
- System monitoring challenges

**OrgSphere Value**:
- Built-in security features
- Pre-built integrations
- Automated user management
- Comprehensive monitoring

---

### 6. Finance Manager

**Role**: Manages budgets and expenses.

**Goals**:
- Control expenses
- Process reimbursements
- Track budgets
- Ensure compliance

**Pain Points**:

**OrgSphere Value**:
