# Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Models

OrgSphere offers two purchasing models:

1. **Cloud SaaS**: Subscription based on organization size tier
2. **Self-Hosted License**: One-time perpetual license with custom domain deployment

---

## Cloud SaaS Tiers

Flat monthly pricing based on organization size. No per-seat charges. Predictable costs.

### Free

**Target**: Evaluation, very small teams

**Price**: $0/month

**Organization Size**: Up to 10 members

**Features**:
- Organization Graph (view only)
- Employee profiles
- Basic leave tracking
- Community support

**Limits**:
- 1 admin user
- OrgSphere branding
- No API access
- 1 GB storage
- No custom domain

---

### Starter

**Target**: Small businesses

**Price**: $49/month

**Organization Size**: 11-50 members

**Features**:
- Full Organization Graph (edit)
- Employee Management
- Leave Management
- Basic Attendance
- Basic Approvals
- Email support

**Limits**:
- 2 admin users
- 5 custom leave types
- Basic analytics

## Self-Hosted License

One-time perpetual license for organizations that want full control.

### License Tiers

| Tier | Price | Members | Features |
|------|-------|---------|----------|
| Starter | $999 | Up to 50 | Core modules |
| Business | $2,999 | Up to 500 | All modules |
| Enterprise | $7,999 | Unlimited | All modules + source |

### What's Included

- Perpetual license (no expiry)
- Source code access
- Custom domain deployment
- Self-managed updates
- Community support
- 1 year of updates included

### Optional Add-Ons

| Add-On | Price | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Updates extension | $499/year | Continued updates after first year |
| Priority support | $999/year | Email + chat support |
| Premium support | $2,999/year | Dedicated engineer |
| Custom development | $200/hour | Feature development |

### Fair Use Policy

Self-hosted license grants:

- Use within your organization
- Deploy on your infrastructure
- Custom domain configuration
- Internal modifications allowed

Prohibited:

- Reselling as a service
- Sublicensing to third parties
- Public hosting for others
- Trademark usage

### Deployment Options

- Docker Compose
- Kubernetes Helm chart
- Manual installation
- Terraform modules

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. BambooHR ($6-12/seat/mo)

- BambooHR: Per-seat, adds up at scale
- OrgSphere: Flat tier, predictable

### vs. Workday ($100+/user/mo)

- Workday: Enterprise pricing, complex
- OrgSphere: Simple tiers, transparent

### vs. GitLab (Free + Premium + Ultimate)

- Similar dual model (SaaS + self-hosted)
- OrgSphere: Org-focused, not dev-focused

### vs. ERPNext (Free + $50/org/mo)

- ERPNext: General ERP
- OrgSphere: Org-native graph
