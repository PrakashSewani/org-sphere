# Product Goals

## Primary Goals

OrgSphere helps organizations:

1. **Understand their structure** - Visualize the entire organization as a connected graph
2. **Operate their workforce** - Manage employees, attendance, leave, and approvals
3. **Visualize relationships** - See reporting lines, team connections, and organizational patterns
4. **Reduce manual HR work** - Automate workflows, approvals, and routine tasks
5. **Enable self-service** - Let employees manage their own profiles, requests, and information
6. **Provide executive insights** - Real-time analytics on organizational health
7. **Improve communication** - Targeted messaging based on organizational structure
8. **Support organizational growth** - Scale from startup to enterprise
